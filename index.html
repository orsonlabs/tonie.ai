The premise

Deserts hold some of the strongest, steadiest wind on Earth, and almost none of the infrastructure. We build the intelligence that lets energy run where people rarely can.

    The system

    One loop, running every second.

    1. Sense

      Satellite weather, met masts, turbine SCADA and battery telemetry flow into one stream.

    2. Predict

      Each site's wind and output are forecast with uncertainty you can plan around.

    3. Route

      Power is dispatched between generation, storage and the loads that matter most.

    4. Learn

      Every decision is compared with what really happened, and the models improve.

    Conditions
